# Contributor Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience level, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or other personal characteristics.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, inclusive, healthy, and professional community.

## Expected Behavior

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive community include:

- Demonstrating empathy, patience, and respect toward other people
- Respecting differing opinions, experiences, and viewpoints
- Giving and receiving constructive feedback professionally
- Accepting responsibility for mistakes and working to correct them
- Respecting stated boundaries and requests to stop unwelcome behavior
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its community
- Helping create an environment where people can learn and contribute safely
- Discussing technical and security concerns without attacking individuals
- Handling sensitive security information responsibly

## Unacceptable Behavior

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Harassment, intimidation, discrimination, or threats
- Sexualized language, imagery, or unwanted attention
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, or personal attacks
- Repeatedly disrupting discussions or refusing to respect stated boundaries
- Deliberate misgendering or use of rejected names
- Publishing another person’s private information without permission
- Sharing credentials, private infrastructure details, audit records, or sensitive security information without authorization
- Encouraging or conducting unauthorized testing against systems, networks, or accounts
- Pressuring someone to disclose private or identifying information
- Coordinated harassment or encouraging others to target an individual
- Retaliating against someone who reports a concern or participates in an investigation
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community

Disagreement is not itself unacceptable behavior. Technical ideas, security assumptions, code, and documentation may be challenged directly, but criticism must remain focused on the work rather than the person.

## Security Research and Responsible Conduct

This project involves security tooling. All community members must act responsibly and lawfully.

Do not:

- Test this project against systems or networks without explicit authorization
- Post live credentials, tokens, personal information, private host details, or operational audit logs
- Publish vulnerability details before coordinated disclosure is complete
- Use project discussions to request or promote harmful, destructive, or unauthorized activity
- Present experimental functionality as safe for production use
- Encourage contributors to bypass the project’s enforced authorization boundaries

Security research, proof-of-concept material, and technical discussion must remain proportionate, authorized, and relevant to improving the project.

Suspected vulnerabilities must be reported according to [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not through a public issue, discussion, or pull request.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project community spaces, including:

- GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and code reviews
- Commit messages and documentation
- Project-related correspondence
- Other online or offline spaces where someone is officially representing the project

It also applies when an individual uses an official project identity, speaks on behalf of the project, or represents the project at an event.

Conduct outside project spaces may be considered when it creates a credible safety concern for community members or materially affects participation in the project.

## Reporting a Conduct Concern

Do not report sensitive conduct concerns through a public issue, discussion, pull request, or comment.

Use the repository’s confidential reporting channel:

[Submit a private report](https://github.com/backyard-labs/kali-mcp-security-lab/security/advisories/new)

When possible, include:

- A description of what happened
- Where and when it occurred
- Links, screenshots, or other relevant context
- Whether the behavior is continuing
- Any immediate safety or privacy concerns
- The outcome you are requesting, if you are comfortable providing one

Do not investigate the matter yourself, confront the reported person on behalf of the project, or publish confidential details.

If the report concerns a project maintainer or the private reporting channel is unavailable, open a minimal public issue requesting an alternative private contact method. Do not include the underlying allegations or sensitive details in that issue.

Reports will be handled as confidentially as reasonably possible. Information may be shared only when necessary to investigate, protect participants, comply with legal obligations, or enforce this Code of Conduct.

Knowingly false or malicious reports may themselves violate this Code of Conduct. A report that cannot be substantiated is not automatically considered false or malicious.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for interpreting and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions, comments, commits, code, issues, pull requests, discussions, and other participation that does not align with this Code of Conduct. They may also temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Enforcement decisions should consider:

- The nature, context, and impact of the behavior
- Whether the conduct was isolated or repeated
- Whether a stated boundary was ignored
- Whether the person accepted responsibility and attempted repair
- Risks to affected individuals and the wider community
- The need for proportional, consistent action

Maintainers must avoid conflicts of interest. A maintainer who is directly involved in a report should not make the final enforcement decision when another appropriate reviewer is available.

Retaliation against reporters, witnesses, affected individuals, or people participating in enforcement is prohibited.

## Enforcement Guidelines

Maintainers may apply the following measures depending on the severity, frequency, context, and impact of the conduct.

### 1. Informal Correction

**Community impact:** Minor inappropriate behavior or an isolated lapse in judgment.

**Possible response:** A private explanation of the concern, clarification of expected behavior, or request to edit or remove problematic content.

### 2. Formal Warning

**Community impact:** A clear violation of community standards or repeated behavior after an informal correction.

**Possible response:** A written warning describing the violation, required corrective action, and consequences of continued behavior.

### 3. Temporary Restriction

**Community impact:** A serious violation, sustained inappropriate behavior, retaliation, or refusal to respect community boundaries.

**Possible response:** Temporary restriction from project interaction, including issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, or other communication.

### 4. Permanent Restriction

**Community impact:** Severe misconduct or a sustained pattern of harassment, threats, discrimination, retaliation, or other harmful behavior.

**Possible response:** Permanent removal from project participation and community spaces under the maintainers’ control.

The enforcement process is intended to protect the community and restore safe participation, not to provide a guaranteed sequence of escalating steps. Serious conduct may result in immediate temporary or permanent restriction.

## Privacy and Transparency

The project will not publicly identify a reporter or affected person without permission unless disclosure is legally required.

Public enforcement notices, when appropriate, should disclose only the information necessary to explain the project’s action and protect the community.

No specific response or resolution timeframe is guaranteed. Reports will be handled on a best-effort basis appropriate to this experimental, maintainer-run project.
